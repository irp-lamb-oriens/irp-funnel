# Tajikistan Country Guide

## Used Household Goods and Personal Effects

### Documents Required

- Copy of passport
- Copy of visa (diplomats and foreigners)
- Letter of employment
- Packing list
- Original bill of lading (OBL) / air waybill (AWB)
- Diplomatic card (diplomats)

### Specific Information

Do not send insurance or any other documents showing the value of household goods and personal effects with the shipment to avoid the payment of heavy import duties.

## Motor Vehicles

### Documents Required

- Copy of passport
- Copy of visa (foreigners)
- Copy of diplomatic card (diplomats)
- Employment confirmation (foreigners)
- OBL / AWB
- Pro-forma invoice
- Original technical passport

### Specific Information

- Vehicles can be imported duty free on a temporary basis.
- Permanent import is subject to tax and duty per Customs tariff.

## Pets

### Documents Required

- Certificate of vaccination
- Veterinary health certificate

### Specific Information

Permission from the embassy required.

## Restricted/Dutiable Items

- Wine and spirits for personal consumption (2 L per person)
- The following items may qualify for free import within the limits indicated:
    - Cigarettes (200)
    - Cigars (50)
    - Tobacco (500g)

## Prohibited Items

- Weapons of all kinds
- Pornography
- Narcotics
- Toxic and radioactive materials

## Consignment Instructions

Recommended: Contact the destination agent to ensure all requirements have been met prior to import, especially for differences regarding air / sea shipments.
