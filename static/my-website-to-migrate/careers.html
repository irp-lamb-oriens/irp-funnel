Regional Business Development Manager | Relocation, and Global Mobility

Location: Bogota, Colombia

Language Requirements:

English: 90%
Spanish: 90%
Portugues: Desirable

​Job Summary:

IRP has a new and exciting opportunity available for a natural sales and relationship developer.

This position is a sales-driven opportunity, with a core focus on identifying and developing new business opportunities with multinational corporations.

The successful Business Development & Sales Manager will have maintained a successful corporate network across the expatriate management sector and will have relevant industry association links such as; WWERC, FEM, IAM, SHRM, etc.

Duties will include but will not be limited to:

  • Researching and identifying talent mobility decision-makers within multinational organizations
  • Building and developing professional relationships, fostering and covering these into new client business
  • Staying up to date on industry trends and techniques, utilizing knowledge to understand client requirements and present the most suitable and appropriate mobility solutions
  • Developing a prospect client portfolio and using strategic sales and business development techniques to convert these into clients
  • Using economic review and assessment to identify potential business with both active and future global talent mobility needs
  • Participating in the creation and delivery of RFPs, contract negotiation and deal closures
  • Regularly attending round-table events, conferences and networking events for brand representation and client development
  • Supporting the creation and delivery of thought leadership publications, white papers, and marketing material

Skills and experience required:

  • Good knowledge of the global talent mobility and HR/expatriate sector
  • Ability to identify decision-makers within HR/Mobility departments
  • Excellent sales and relationship development skills
  • Proven background in sales, client engagement, and business development within global mobility
  • Excellent presentation and communication skills
  • Ability to work remotely from home and travel as required
