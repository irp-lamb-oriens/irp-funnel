Cookie Policy

This website does not store any information that would, on its own, allow us to identify individual users of this service without their permission or explicit input such as entering order information or completing a form on the website.

The cookies in the Categories other than “Necessary” may collect data considered personally identifiable information when either (1) entered directly by you such as inputting your name or email in a form, or (2) when aggregated with other known data by a service such as Google Analytics.

The “Necessary” cookie category also contains first party cookies created and managed by us to operate our private analytics server which does not aggregate your information with third party services. Our private analytics server may collect personally identifiable information submitted by you and also collects information regarding your usage of the site, IP, location and other metrics for us to improve our content and services.

The cookies in the Categories other than “Necessary” are set for users located in the EU (or for users subject to GDPR) only after the user has given consent. For users in the EU or subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) may retract their consent by clicking on the cogwheel in the bottom of the screen to access optional cookie settings and may request that their personal information be deleted or provided to them by submitting a request to info@internationalrelocationpartner.net These cookies are enabled for all other users.

This information is used to better understand how the service is used, to improve our products and services, to increase the relevancy of our marketing and improve internal reporting for making business decisions.
