Spain

Spain is a country that captures the essence of old Europe, with its cobblestone alleys, medieval walls, and castles. It’s a country that exudes history, beauty, and a laidback lifestyle that is rich in every standard other than financial. The people of Spain are friendly, highly conscious of their illustrious history, and what makes the country a magical destination to retire to.

With nearly 3,100 miles of coastline, Spain offers fantastic variety in terrain, culture, and lifestyle. From its rocky, rugged Atlantic coasts to its green hills, winding country roads, and bustling cities, Spain has something for everyone. It’s easy to get around the country thanks to its rapid public transportation, high-speed and suburban train lines, and extensive bus and metro services. Spain has three international airports servicing many airlines, connecting the country with the rest of Europe.

Spain has a rich history, with the Phoenicians, Greeks, Romans, and Arabs all settling here and leaving ample remains, language, and customs. You can walk in the steps of sultans at Granada’s Alhambra, take the pilgrimage route to Santiago de Compostela, or gaze on Segovia’s mighty Roman aqueduct, over 2,000 years old. You’ll find plenty of cities in Spain, large and small, which merit exploration.

Spain is known for its lively, outgoing lifestyle and some of the best food and wine around. Life in Spain is meant to be enjoyed, and hanging out is an art. You can choose beaches, mountains, deserts, rural farms, quaint pueblos, or intensely urban cities. It all comes at an affordable price, making Spain one of the lowest cost-of-living countries in Europe.

Spain is a safe country, with low crime rates and violent crime being rare. The country is among the best in the world for healthcare, with four doctors for every 1,000 people and a life expectancy of 81.92 years. Spain does more organ transplants than any other nation in the world.
